Our Story

Built for the Nights You Read Past Midnight

Vow & Vice began with a simple ache — the way a certain kind of story lodges itself under your ribs and stays there. The candlelit libraries. The vows made in ruin. The quiet, dangerous kind of devotion that only exists in the best romances and the oldest myths.

We didn't want merchandise. We wanted clothing that felt like that ache — heirloom detailing, antique gold, deep botanical color, fabric that feels like it already has a history. Every piece is designed the way a favorite passage is written: with intention, restraint, and just enough danger.
