Shipping

Shipping & Delivery

Once your piece has finished production, standard shipping takes 3–6 business days within the continental U.S. International timelines vary by destination — you'll see an estimate at checkout.
Yes. We ship worldwide. Duties and import taxes, where applicable, are the responsibility of the customer and are calculated at checkout.
Yes — a tracking link is emailed to you the moment your order leaves our production partner.
Production Times

Made to Order

Nothing sits in a warehouse waiting to be sold. Each garment is printed and finished only after you place your order, which is what allows us to work in small batches instead of mass production.
Please allow 5–8 business days for production before your order ships. During new launches or high-demand periods, this window may extend slightly — we'll always note it on the product page if so.
Print-on-Demand

How Our Pieces Are Made

Yes — every piece is produced through a dedicated print-on-demand partner using premium blanks and archival-quality inks. This lets us avoid overproduction while still holding a high bar for fabric weight, print detail, and finish.
Not in the way people often assume. We choose heavyweight fleece and combed cotton blanks and hand-select every print finish, so the result feels considered rather than mass-manufactured.
Returns

Returns & Exchanges

Because each piece is made to order, we accept returns only for sizing exchanges or manufacturing defects, within 14 days of delivery. Please reach out through our Contact page before sending anything back.
We're sorry to hear that. Email us photos of the item and packaging within 7 days of delivery and we'll arrange a replacement at no cost.
Sizing

Fit & Sizing

Yes, with a relaxed, unisex fit throughout the collection. Full chest and length measurements are listed on every product page — if you're between sizes, we recommend sizing down for a closer fit.
Each category has its own measurement chart, since fleece and cotton drape differently. Check the Sizing Guide on the specific product you're considering for the most accurate fit.
Care

Care Instructions

Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colors, and avoid bleach. This protects both the fabric and the printed artwork.
Tumble dry low, or hang dry for the longest possible life of the print. If you need to iron, always do so on the reverse side, and never directly over the artwork.
