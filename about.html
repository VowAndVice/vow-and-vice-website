Our Story

For the Stories We Dream Of

& the ones that ruin us — a little about why Vow & Vice exists.

There is a particular kind of person who finishes a story and doesn't want to leave it. Who closes the cover, or the tab, or the final page, and still feels the candlelight on their skin — the old libraries, the vows made too fast, the villains who were really just the misunderstood. Vow & Vice was built for that person. It was built for us.

Not Merchandise. A Mood.

We didn't want to make clothing that referenced a title on a shelf. We wanted to make clothing that felt like the feeling itself — the ache of romantasy, the hush of dark academia, the glint of a sword half-hidden in roses. Every piece we design starts with a mood board of antique engravings, celestial maps, and heirloom jewelry long before it ever touches a sketch of a garment. The story comes first. The clothing follows.

Slow, On Purpose

Nothing at Vow & Vice is mass-produced. Each piece is made to order, in small batches, so that what you receive feels considered rather than churned out. It takes longer. We think it's worth it — the same way a favorite book is worth rereading slowly, underlining the parts that matter.

Worn, Not Worshipped

You don't need to have read a particular series to wear Vow & Vice. You just need to know the feeling — the pull toward something romantic and a little dangerous, something old and gilded and slightly unwell. If that's you, welcome. We saved you a seat by the fire.
